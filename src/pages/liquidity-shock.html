SCOPE

This is a descriptive study of ten Nasdaq securities over 32 target sessions using XNAS MBP-10 displayed-book data. It does not observe hidden liquidity, individual order identities or queue position; spread and depth shocks are detected separately and are not assumed to represent economically equivalent severity, and follow-up is limited to 120 seconds.
